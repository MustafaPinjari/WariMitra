# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - WariMitra

## 1. Project Overview
WariMitra is a unified digital platform designed to digitally empower and coordinate every stakeholder involved in the Wari pilgrimage in Maharashtra, India. It transitions a centuries-old tradition into a connected ecosystem without compromising its spiritual essence.

## 2. Problem Statement
The Wari pilgrimage involves millions of devotees traveling hundreds of kilometers over weeks. Management relies on fragmented communication, manual coordination, and isolated systems. This leads to:
- Delayed emergency response
- Poor crowd management
- Uneven resource distribution (food, water)
- Missing person incidents
- Lack of real-time communication

## 3. Business Value & Objectives
WariMitra aims to provide a single intelligent platform that acts as the "digital operating system of the Wari".
- **Safety:** Reduce emergency response times via real-time reporting.
- **Coordination:** Create seamless collaboration between pilgrims, volunteers, NGOs, and authorities.
- **Resource Optimization:** Ensure equitable distribution of food, water, and medical supplies.
- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Enable predictive planning through AI and real-time operational insights.
- **Digital Inclusion:** Provide simple, voice-first, offline-first mobile applications for rural and elderly users.

## 4. Stakeholder Profiles
- **Pilgrims (Warkaris):** Primary users. Require navigation, safety, facilities mapping, and SOS. High variance in digital literacy.
- **Volunteers:** Backbone of ground operations. Handle incident reporting and ground verification.
- **Dindi Leaders:** Manage organized groups, attendance, and member tracking.
- **Medical Teams:** Provide emergency and routine healthcare. Need patient tracking and ambulance dispatch.
- **Police & Traffic:** Ensure public safety, manage road closures, and find missing persons.
- **NGOs:** Distribute resources like food and water. Need inventory and demand tracking.
- **Government Administration:** Oversee the entire event through a centralized command dashboard with GIS maps and AI predictions.

## 5. Success Criteria (KPIs)
- **Pilgrims:** Faster access to services, reduced confusion, increased satisfaction.
- **Volunteers:** Faster task assignment, improved communication.
- **Government:** Real-time situational awareness, predictive decision-making, 50% reduction in emergency response time.

## 6. Long-Term Vision
Establish WariMitra as a scalable, national digital public infrastructure that can be customized for other large-scale mass gatherings, such as the Kumbh Mela and Amarnath Yatra.
